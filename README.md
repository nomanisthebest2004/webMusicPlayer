# Iota Music Player

A simple web-based music player for playing local music files directly in your browser.

## Features

* 🎵 **Play Local Music** — Drag and drop your music files into the player to start listening.
* 💿 **Custom Disc Artwork** — Drag and drop any image to use it as the artwork displayed on the disc.
* 🖥️ **Browser-Based** — No installation or music upload required; your local files are processed directly in the browser.

## Usage

1. Open the music player.
2. Drag and drop a music file into the player.
3. The music will be loaded and ready to play.
4. Drag and drop an image to set the disc artwork.

> Your music files remain local to your device and are not uploaded to a server.
